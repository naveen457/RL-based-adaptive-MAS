## 3. Methodological Transparency & Integrity
- **Simulated DAG Proxy:** Token and cost metrics are explicitly disclosed as simulated critical-path DAG estimates ($650\text{ tok/node} + 150\text{ tok/edge}$), not actual commercial LLM provider bills.
- **Deterministic Proxy:** Neural adaptation is disclosed as a deterministic capability compatibility proxy, involving no live LLM calls.
- **Separation of Contributions:** The experimental neural policy is strictly decoupled from the live runtime Q-learning LangGraph controller.
- **Negative Findings:** Preserved and explained with structural ablation of the action alphabet.
